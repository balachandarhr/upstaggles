How to play

  • Move: joystick (left thumb) · WASD / arrow keys
  • Jump: JUMP button · Space (hold for higher) – press again in the air to double jump
  • Dash / roll: DASH button · Shift or K (also breaks enemies)
  • Spin attack: SPIN button · E or J
  • Camera: drag the right side of the screen / mouse · Z and X keys
  • Climb: walk into green vines
  • Bosses: dodge the attacks; when the boss is dizzy (★ ring) hit it with spin, dash or a stomp
  • Pause: ❚❚ button · Esc / P
